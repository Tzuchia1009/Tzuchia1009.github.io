Research, training

Tzu-Chia Huang is an adjunct lecturer and Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering at Tamkang University. Research and teaching cover generative AI, multimodal intelligence and intelligent systems.

With an academic background in finance and history, Huang previously served as a senior project manager at Tamkang University’s Center for Ocean and Undersea Technology. Work included industry–academia research, cross-disciplinary project coordination and professional training in underwater cultural heritage.

Professional activities include course and workshop planning, practical instruction, requirements clarification and project coordination, working with partners on specific educational and industry needs.
