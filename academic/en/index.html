ABOUT TZU-CHIA

Academic background

Computer science methods.
A perspective from history.

My academic background spans finance, history and computer science. I am an adjunct lecturer in the Departments of Computer Science and Information Engineering and History at Tamkang University, working on research, teaching and applications in generative and multimodal AI.

Previously, I served as a senior project manager at Tamkang University’s Center for Ocean and Undersea Technology. Across six years, I helped manage and deliver more than 40 industry–academia research projects and supported professional training in underwater cultural heritage.
